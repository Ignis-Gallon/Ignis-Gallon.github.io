# What Are Design Patterns?

Designing S/W presents challenges that need a reliable and reusable solution.

- Discussed design patterns in S/W development were popularized by the gang of four authors in their book “Design patterns”
- A design pattern helps us identify a problem and presents a tested solution
- A design pattern must be reusable and must produce a reliable outcome
- Developers and consumers benefit from a clean interface and documentation that promotes sharing code between teams
